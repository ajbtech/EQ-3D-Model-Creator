EQ 3D Model Creator

Turn your EverQuest / P99 character into a posed, print-ready 3D model — entirely in your browser. No game art leaves your machine.

1 Upload your model

Export it yourself with LanternExtractor (set ExportGltfInGlbFormat=true and ExportAllAnimationFrames=true). Whatever gear is in the model gets posed and printed too.

2 Character

Sets the printed figurine height.

3 Pose

Upload a model to choose a pose.

4 Equipment (optional)

Weapons/shields export to LanternExtractor's equipment/ folder. Nudge the sliders to seat it in the hand.

5 Make it printable

Advanced
How to print this STL
  • Scale: already scaled to a tabletop height for the chosen race — tweak in your slicer if you like.
  • Supports: generate them in your slicer (Cura / PrusaSlicer / Lychee). Action poses (attacking, casting) need them.
  • Material: resin for fine figurine detail; FDM for larger, cheaper prints.
  • Heads-up: the pipeline guarantees a watertight, manifold solid, but no design has been physically test-printed yet — sanity-check before a long print.
